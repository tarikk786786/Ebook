## 2. Search Intent Classification & On-Page Target Rules

1. **Transactional Product Pages (`/book/[slug]`):**
   - Title format: `[Title] by [Author] | AnonTrove Digital Bookstore`
   - Primary CTA: Instant UPI Checkout (₹29 - ₹199), Immediate In-Browser Reading.
2. **Category Hubs (`/category/[slug]`):**
   - Title format: `[Category Name] eBooks | AnonTrove`
   - Content: Deep topic overview, curated bestsellers, and structured FAQ answering key questions.
3. **Keyword Cannibalization Prevention:**
   - Every individual book targets its exact title, subtitle, and author combination.
   - Broad generic head terms (e.g., "programming books") are reserved exclusively for Category Hubs.
