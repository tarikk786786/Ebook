## 2. Content & Keyword Opportunity Gaps

1. **DRM-Free Micro-Transactions (₹29 - ₹99):**
   Mainstream competitors charge ₹500 - ₹2,000 per ebook. High search intent exists for "affordable programming ebooks pdf", "stock market price action guide inr", and "drm free tech manuals".
2. **Instant UPI Integration:**
   International platforms require credit cards with international payment enablement. AnonTrove leverages native FamPay / FamGateway dynamic UPI QR codes with 0% surcharge for seamless mobile checkout.
3. **Integrated Browser Reading:**
   Competitors force users to download external apps. AnonTrove provides instant, sandboxed in-browser reading immediately upon transaction completion.
